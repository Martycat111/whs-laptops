Getting there

You may have some questions about picking up.
This page is here to help you with that.

First, when?

The time is picked in the collection part of the form.
This is at interval, Lunch or Afterschool.

What do I do once I'm there?

A person should be waiting there to help you,
but only at the arranged time. This should be one of these:

  • 10:40 if interval is selected.
  • 1:20 if lunch is selected.
  • 3:20 if afterschool is selected.
  • Or a custom time between 10:40 - 11:00, 1:20 - 2:20, & 3:20 - 3:30

The person will wait for 10min

If you missed the timeframe, please email wallal26192@whs.school.nz to schedule a new time
